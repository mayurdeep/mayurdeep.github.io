about

I am a graduate student at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, in the Visual Information Processing Lab with Prof. Rajiv Soundararajan.

I work on reconstructing scenes that move. Given a few synchronized videos of an event, the aim is a three-dimensional record of it that can be watched from any viewpoint, at any moment. Most of my time goes into the parts that move, which are, inconveniently, the parts people look at.

Broadly: 3D vision, dynamic scene reconstruction, novel view synthesis, and image and video processing.

The cow is made of a few dozen 3D Gaussians. Physicists assume a spherical cow; we have moved on.
