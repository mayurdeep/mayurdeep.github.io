01What I'm curious about

Capturing a world that won't sit still.

I'm an M.Tech (Research) student in the Visual Information Processing Lab at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, advised by Prof. Rajiv Soundararajan.

My work is on dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting: given several synchronized video cameras looking at a moving scene, build a 3D model that can render it from viewpoints no camera ever saw. Static backgrounds are mostly solved. The hard part is people, hands, objects in motion, and that is exactly where today's methods go soft and blurry.

How do we spend a model's capacity where the motion is, and keep it there for minutes, not seconds?
