I am an M.Tech (Research) student at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, advised by Prof. Rajiv Soundararajan in the Visual Information Processing Lab. I work on 3D reconstruction of dynamic scenes: building 3D models from multi-view videos that can be viewed from new viewpoints, with a focus on keeping moving regions sharp, including over long videos.

I grew up in Dibrugarh, Assam, a town known for its tea. Before IISc, I did my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering at Jorhat Engineering College. At IISc, I also served as Secretary of the IISc ACM Student Chapter.

I love movies, and filmmaking is something I am genuinely drawn to. I hope to work in film one day; until then, 3D vision feels like the next best thing. In that spirit, here is something I like to share: The Campanile Movie, a short film Paul Debevec made in 1997 from a handful of photographs of a clock tower, long before NeRFs and Gaussian splats.

Outside of work, I enjoy travelling, motorbiking and cooking (I am a fairly good cook).

I am always happy to talk about 3D vision, films, or anything else. Feel free to reach out by email.
